\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\subsection{What was built}

Algorithm~\ref{alg:gfp} is implemented. The shipping engine is untouched: the prototype is a
\emph{copy} of the engine's search file plus one added routine, linked into a separate binary that
takes every other core source unchanged. The harness \texttt{gfp\_proto <model> <Proc.Loc>
<Proc.Loc>} runs \texttt{p --> q} through \textbf{both} the shipping \texttt{leads\_to\_net} and the
new \texttt{leads\_to\_gfp} and compares the verdicts. Verdict disagreement is the refutation the
harness exists to find. Local edges, binary synchronisation and broadcast are all supported; a
transition is represented as the \emph{list} of participating edges, which gives $\Pre$ one code
path for all three.

\subsection{Differential testing}

Every ordered pair of locations of one representative process, across
\texttt{fischer4/5/6}, \texttt{twolock4/5}, \texttt{arbiter4/5}, \texttt{viking4},
\texttt{phil4/6}, \texttt{grc4}, run through both engines:

\begin{center}
\textbf{164 agree \quad 0 disagree}
\end{center}
\measured{}

Reported rather than hidden: \texttt{arbiter6} liveness \textbf{did not terminate} in 15 minutes
under the \emph{reference} engine and was killed; its reachability finishes instantly at 256 states.
Recorded as incomplete, counted as nothing. An earlier sweep excluded \texttt{csma} (broadcast
unimplemented at the time) and flagged four \texttt{grc4} runs \textsc{unusable} against a
federation-width cap of $256$; broadcast is now implemented and raising the cap to $4096$ makes
\texttt{grc4} converge at width $1.00$, so both exclusions have been lifted and the individual runs
verified.

\subsection{What the differential testing actually caught}
\label{sec:eval:bugs}

Five defects, all in the prototype, all found by disagreement rather than inspection. The failure
modes are the informative part.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{No ``let time pass forever'' witness.} First sweep: 6 agree / 78 disagree, every one
        in the safe direction. A maximal $\lnot q$ run may take \emph{no} discrete transition at
        all. Adding the divergence test took it to 94/2.
  \item \textbf{Missing pre-action delay in $\Pre$.} A stored zone is delay-closed as a set, so the
        forward operator may write $\Zn \cap g$; an individual valuation may need to delay first.
  \item \textbf{Per-valuation stuck test.} Produced a false violation on \texttt{arbiter4}. Cause
        (Remark~\ref{rem:correction}): the active-clock reduction, \emph{not} the $LU$ abstraction,
        which is what we first blamed.
  \item \textbf{Active-clock reduction not inverted in $\Pre$}
        (\S\ref{sec:liveness:activeclock}). Over-constrained $\Pre$, collapsing the fixpoint and
        \emph{losing} real counterexamples on \texttt{phil4/6}, \texttt{grc4} and
        \texttt{arbiter4/5}. Fixing it took the sweep from 135/10 to 164/0.
  \item \textbf{No re-expansion after a zone is enlarged}, and a self-loop that cleared the
        adjacency of the node being expanded.
\end{enumerate}

Every disagreement encountered at any stage was in the \emph{safe} direction --- the prototype
missing a violation --- except defect 3. Where prototype and reference engine disagreed,
\verifyta{} was consulted as tiebreak, and in every such case it sided with the reference engine.

\subsection{Query coverage: the same fixpoint decides \texttt{E[]} and \texttt{A<>}}
\label{sec:eval:coverage}

Algorithm~\ref{alg:gfp} is not specific to leads-to. It computes a greatest fixpoint, so it decides
the whole \emph{greatest-fixpoint half} of the logic:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
query & fixpoint & status here \\
\midrule
\texttt{E<> $\varphi$} / \texttt{A[] $\varphi$} & least (plain reachability) & already at the floor; the gfp adds nothing \\
\texttt{E[] $\varphi$} & $\nu W.\,\varphi \wedge (\mathrm{stop} \vee \Pre W)$ & \textbf{decided}, 51/51 agreement \\
\texttt{A<> $\varphi$} & $\lnot\,$\texttt{E[]}$\,\lnot\varphi$ & immediate from the above \\
\texttt{p --> q} & $\mathsf{EF}(p \wedge \mathsf{EG}\lnot q)$ & \textbf{decided}, 164/164 agreement \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\measured{}

51 \texttt{E[]} queries --- every location of a representative process across
\texttt{fischer4/5/6}, \texttt{twolock4/5}, \texttt{arbiter4/5}, \texttt{phil4/6},
\texttt{csma4/5}, \texttt{viking4}, \texttt{grc4} --- checked against the engine's
\texttt{exists\_invariant\_run}: \textbf{51 agree, 0 disagree}.

\begin{remark}[The verdict extraction is where this goes wrong, not the fixpoint]
The first attempt got 20/34 and every failure was an \emph{unsound} ``satisfied''. The fixpoint was
correct; the \emph{verdict rule} was not. \texttt{p --> q} asks whether \emph{some reachable}
$p \wedge \lnot q$ state has a non-empty gfp, i.e.\ $\mathsf{EF}(p \wedge \mathsf{EG}\lnot q)$;
\texttt{E[] $\varphi$} asks whether \emph{the initial state} does, i.e.\ $\mathsf{EG}\varphi$ at
$s_0$. Reusing the first rule for the second computes $\mathsf{EF}\,\mathsf{EG}\,\varphi$, which is
strictly weaker, and it produced ``satisfied'' on every predicate that does not hold initially ---
contradicted by \verifyta{} on \texttt{fischer4} \texttt{E[] P1.wait}, \texttt{arbiter4}
\texttt{E[] C1.want}, \texttt{phil4} \texttt{E[] P0.HoldL} and \texttt{viking4}
\texttt{E[] Viking1.safe}. Restricting the check to the root gives 51/51. \measured{}
\end{remark}

\subsection{The reachability side}

The deferral policy of \S\ref{sec:deferral} was also implemented, on a barrier-free global FIFO,
and measured at parking budgets $D \in \{0,1,2,4,8,16\}$ on four models. It saves \textbf{exactly
zero} expansions at every budget --- the columns are identical, not merely close. See
\S\ref{sec:deferral} and Proposition~\ref{prop:uniform}.

\subsection{What has not been measured}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Speed.} No wall-clock comparison is reported. The state counts are the claim.
  \item \textbf{Parallel scaling.} Phase 3 is implemented sequentially
        (\S\ref{sec:liveness:parallel}). Order-independence is \proved{}; speedup is \openq{}.
  \item \textbf{Larger instances.} \texttt{arbiter6} and above are out of reach of the
        \emph{reference} engine, which is what the harness compares against, so the differential
        method cannot be pushed further without a different oracle.
  \item \textbf{\texttt{grc4}'s $18.32$ zones/disc.} The one family where Phase 1 does not reach the
        floor. Why it does not, and whether it matters, is unexamined.
\end{itemize}
